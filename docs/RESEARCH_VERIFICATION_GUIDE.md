links below were independently checked while improving the search strategy on
4 November 2026. They are not inserted as hard-coded winners, candidates,
financial figures or answers in the code. The application must discover and
inspect actual URLs at run time from the raw incoming customer enquiry.

### Handling apparently conflicting claims

The FlytBase SQM case-study headline/metadata and text can use different
formulations of outcomes. Prefer the complete narrative, check the source's
publication/date and original wording, and avoid a guaranteed ROI or applying
figures from an iodine-site inspection project to the proposed lithium sites.

### What the workflow should accomplish

1. Search from the **incoming enquiry only**, without injecting the links above into prompts or selecting a winner by name.
2. Inspect original sources and preserve source IDs, URLs and verbatim passages.
3. Surface SQM's previously published FlytBase relationship and ask **which site/division** the new enquiry concerns.
4. Compare at least an on-company example and a referral-associated example when both are actually discovered.
5. Prefer direct AE ownership when current partner availability, country coverage or responsibilities are not established; request confirmation from FlytBase before assignment.
6. Withhold unsupported financial/reporting claims and clearly label incomplete checks.
